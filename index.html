Singapore Awards Watch

Newly awarded construction contracts in Singapore, with the main contractor to approach and how well the work fits our lifting gear and fabrication scope. Each Monday edition adds what has been announced since the last one.

Newest · reported 1 Oct 2026
Tuas Road Viaduct Phase 2

LTA awarded three viaduct contracts worth about S$1.2bn: CCCC (S$430.3m), China Harbour (S$404.4m) and Hwa Seng Builder (S$381.6m). Elevated structures mean girder and precast handling.

Largest · reported mid-Sep 2026
Tengah General and Community Hospital

Ssangyong E&C won the design-and-build contract for a 1,534-bed campus, reported at US$2.15bn with a 63-month programme including design.

All awards, newest first

114 contracts, one list. Contracts above S$16m come from BCA's official monthly lists (January to August 2026); the rest are from news reports and company announcements. A project reported by both appears once. BCA's lists for Nov and Dec 2025 and Sep and Oct 2026 were not found online, so those months are covered by news reports only. Fit for us is our own rule-of-thumb for lifting gear and fabrication demand: High for heavy civil, tunnels, plants, large industrial and big new builds; Low for upgrading, fit-out, alterations and maintenance. Stainless steel work is outside our scope.
